# Removing a member from the organization

If an employee leaves the company or moves to another role, you may want to remove them from the Ninchat organization, customer service queues, and team channels.

## Jäsenen poistaminen organisaatiosta

Organisaation jäsenten hallinta vaatii organisaation operaattorioikeustasoa.&#x20;

1. Valitse organisaation valikosta Organisaation päänäkymä ja siirry Jäsenet kohtaan.&#x20;
2. Klikkaa listalla poistettavan organisaation jäsenen kohdalla pistevalikkoa ja valitse Hallinnoi jäsenyyttä.
3. Poista jäsen ensin kaikista jonoista ja tämän jälkeen pääset poistamaan jäsenen organisaatiosta.
4. Klikkaa Poista jäsen -painiketta.

## Removing a member from the organization

Managing organization members requires organization operator permissions.

1. From the Organization dashboard, and choose the Members view.
2. In the list, click the three-dot menu for the organization member you want to remove and select Manage membership.
3. First remove the member from all queues. After this, you can remove the member from the organization.
4. Click the "Remove member" button.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (165).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The member will be removed from the organization, channels, and queues.

## Deleting a user account from Ninchat

If the user account needs to be deleted entirely, the member must do this themselves. Instructions are available via the link.
