# Manage queues and queue members

With operator rights, you can view all of your organization's queues in the 'Queues' section of the Organization Settings, access the settings and statistics for each queue, and manage queue members.

### Manage queue members <a href="#manage-queue-members" id="manage-queue-members"></a>

You can manage the members belonging to a specific queue, add and remove them in the 'Queues' section of the Organization dashboard. You can manage an individual user's queues by clicking on the user row in the 'Organization' section.

Manage queue member status for a single user by clicking the agent row in the "Organization" tab.

1\. Choose 'Queues' from the Organization dashboard menu, click the three-dot menu on the right, and choose 'Members'.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (28).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Choose the queue that members you like to manage</p></figcaption></figure></div>

2\. A view opens with a list showing which members of the organization belong to the specific queue. You can add or remove members by checking or unchecking their names.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (30).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Ad or remove organization memebers from the queue.</p></figcaption></figure></div>

### Adding a New Queue

In the Queues view, clicking the "New queue" button will open the "Create a new queue" view. Enter the queue name, select the desired features, and click "Create queue."
