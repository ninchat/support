# Opening and closing queue

## Customer queue states

Customer queue is shown in Sidebar with

* Red dot - Queue is closed
* Green dot - Queue is open
* Blue notice colours - There are customers in queue

In the sidebar, next to the queue name, you can see a clock that indicates how long the (first) customer in the queue has been waiting.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (48).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>The customer queue in the sidebar: Closed (red), open (green), customer in queue (blue).</p></figcaption></figure></div>

### Queueing time <a href="#queueing-time" id="queueing-time"></a>

When there are customers in queue, you can see a timer next to the queue name indicating how long (the first) queueing customer has been waiting.

If queueing time has prolonged for more than one hour, the ∞ infinity symbol is shown instead of time.

### Opening and closing the queue <a href="#opening-a-queue" id="opening-a-queue"></a>

**To open the queue:** Click the arrow icon next to the queue name and select "Open queue" from the menu. Customers can now join the queue.

**To close the queue:** Click the arrow icon next to the queue name and select "Close queue" from the menu. Customers in the queue at the time of closing will remain in the queue until they are picked or they decide to close the chat/window.
