# Removing a member from a channel

### **How to remove a member from a channel**

1. Removing a user from a channel requires moderator rights for that specific channel.\
   Make sure you have moderator rights for the channel. If you are a channel operator, you can enable these rights by clicking your name in the channel member list and selecting _Give moderator rights_.
2. Click the profile picture of the user you want to remove and select _Remove from channel_ from the members card. Then click _OK_ in the confirmation dialog that appears. You can open the members card from the top bar or from the Channel topic members list.
