# Remove user account

{% hint style="info" %}
<mark style="color:$primary;">An organization operator can remove users from the organization and queues.</mark>\ <mark style="color:$primary;">A channel moderator can remove channel members from the channel.</mark>\ <mark style="color:$primary;">Only the user can delete their entire account from Ninchat by him/herself.</mark>
{% endhint %}

### Delete your account <a href="#delete-your-account" id="delete-your-account"></a>

If you wish to delete your Ninchat account entirely, you have to delete all of your login identities.

1. Click the arrow icon next to your username at the bottom of the Sidebar.
2. Select "Settings and profile" from the menu.
3. In your personal settings, go to the tab "Authentication".
4. Click a trash icon to remove an identity, repeat for all identities you have.
5. Log out.

If you wish, you can delete your Ninchat user account by removing all login identities.

* Under **Accounts & password**, go to the advanced settings at the bottom of the page.
* Click **Delete user account**.
