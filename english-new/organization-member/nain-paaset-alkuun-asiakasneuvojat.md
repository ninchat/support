# Getting Started

## Starting as new member <a href="#starting-as-new-agent" id="starting-as-new-agent"></a>

Here are brief instructions on how to get started as a new member. For more detailed guidance, please see the instruction pages available through the links. These instructions are intended for new members whose company or organization already has an existing Ninchat organization.

## Creating an account

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Join Ninchat via invitation link sent to you.

You have received an invitation link via email. Use Chrome and Firefox browsers only.

Open the invitation link by clicking the _Accept invitation_ -button in your email messag

![Kutsuviesti sähköpostissa](<../.gitbook/assets/Kutsu email kanavalle (1).png>)

After opening the link click on the _Accept invite_ -button.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
<mark style="color:$primary;">If your organization uses AD authentication for Ninchat, accepting the invitation will direct you to sign in with your AD credentials.</mark>
{% endhint %}

For more information, see the page _Ninchat invitation:_

{% content-ref url="kayttajatili/ninchat-kutsun-hyvaksyminen.md" %}
[ninchat-kutsun-hyvaksyminen.md](kayttajatili/ninchat-kutsun-hyvaksyminen.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create your account

{% hint style="info" %}
<mark style="color:$primary;">Note! If you already have an account, sign in and skip this step.</mark>
{% endhint %}

Once you have accepted the invitation create your own Ninchat account (if you already have one, sign in).

Enter required information:

* Name: your name
* Email: your email address (pay attention to capitalization)
* Password: create a safe password for your account

Click on _Create account_ to proceed.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

More info on page _Create new account_:

{% content-ref url="kayttajatili/kayttajatilin-luonti.md" %}
[kayttajatilin-luonti.md](kayttajatili/kayttajatilin-luonti.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Permission to show notifications

When you sign in to Ninchat the browser asks to give ninchat.com a permission to show notifications. Allow notifications. Now you will receive notifications of new messages etc.

![Ilmoitusten salliminen eri web-selainohjelmissa](<../.gitbook/assets/Ilmoitusten salliminen web-selaimissa.png>)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Verify your account

You still need to verify your account. In your email you will receive a message that contains a link you need to click on to verify your account.

Use the verification link only once.

![Vahvistusviesti sähköpostissa](<../.gitbook/assets/Please verify your email.png>)

Sign in at: [**https://ninchat.com/new**](https://ninchat.com/new)
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}



## User settings <a href="#kayttajaasetukset" id="kayttajaasetukset"></a>

### 1. User profile <a href="#id-1-kayttajaprofiili" id="id-1-kayttajaprofiili"></a>

Click on the arrow next to your name. From the opening menu choose _Asetukset ja profiili/Settings and profile_.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (173).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Enter your preferred name in th _Screen name_ field and your full legal name in the _Full name_ field.

Upload a profile picture in JPG or PNG format.

{% hint style="info" %}
<mark style="color:$info;">The</mark> <mark style="color:$info;"></mark>_<mark style="color:$info;">Full name</mark>_ <mark style="color:$info;"></mark><mark style="color:$info;">field is not visible to the customer.</mark>
{% endhint %}

Remember to save your changes.

### 2. Notifications

Navigate to the _Settings_ and _Profile_ view, go to _Notifications_.

Ninchat can notify you about events and messages in three ways: with sound, through desktop notifications, and by email. Notifications are also always displayed in Ninchat’s sidebar.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (174).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Ilmoitusasetuksissa on valmiiksi asetettuna hyödyllisimmät ilmoitukset. Lisää tai poista halutut ilmoitukset, esimerkiksi sähköpostiisi.

Voit saada selainilmoitukset (työpöytäilmoitukset) myös Ninchatin ollessa aktiivisena. Klikkaa "Näytä ilmoitukset kun en ole paikalla" Off-asentoon.

Voit myös laittaa "Näytä työpöytäilmoitukset kunnes kuittaan ne" _On-asentoon, jos haluat_ työpöytäilmoitusten jäävän näkyviin. Kuittaa ilmoitus klikkaamalla.

Lisätietoa Käyttäjäasetukset-sivulla:



In this view you can add or remove the notifications you want, for example email notifications.

You can also receive browser notifications (desktop notifications) while Ninchat is active. Set “Display notifications only when I'm idle” to Off.

You can choose if sound and desktop notifications are, or are not enabled when Ninchat window is active. You can also leave desktop notifications visible until you acknowledge them.

For more information, see the _User Settings_ page:

## Channel

Through the invitation, you will gain access to your internal team channel, where you can chat with your colleagues about matters related to chat and other topics. You may also have been invited separately to a technical support channel, where Ninchat staff and your own support contacts provide assistance with chat-related issues.

Through the channel’s member list, you can also start one-to-one private conversations with your teammates. Read more on the following pages:

## Customer queues and chats

Once you have been added as an agent for customer queues, your assigned queues will appear in the left sidebar, where you can access picking customers from the queues.

If you have been granted organization operator permissions, you can also open and close queues, change their settings, and view statistics. Your organization determines and assigns these permissions.
